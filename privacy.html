Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 30, 2026

This policy describes how the Help Me Meow publishing tool ("the app") handles data. The app is a private tool used only by the owner of the Help Me Meow YouTube channel to upload the channel's own videos. It is not offered to the public.

Data the app accesses

With the channel owner's permission, the app uses Google OAuth to obtain the youtube.upload scope, which allows it to upload videos and set their title, description, tags and upload settings on the owner's YouTube channel. It does not read, collect or store any other Google account data.

How data is stored

The OAuth token is stored only on the owner's own computer. It is never sent to any server other than Google's, never shared with third parties and never sold.

Use of Google user data

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The app uses YouTube API Services; see the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time from Google Account permissions.
